# AE Handoff

> **SIMULATION — FICTIONAL OPPORTUNITY**
>
> This document represents how an SDR would hand a qualified opportunity to an Account Executive. It is not based on an actual conversation with Figma or its employees.

---

## 1. Account

**Account:** Figma  
**Industry:** B2B SaaS  
**Sales Category:** Cloud Security / DevSecOps / Security Operations  
**Primary Persona:** Security Engineering  
**Secondary Stakeholders:** Platform Engineering, Cloud Security, Security Leadership

---

## 2. Opportunity Summary

Figma publicly describes significant investment in security investigation and automation.

The simulated prospect conversation indicates that while initial investigation is heavily automated, more complex investigations may still require engineers to manually gather and correlate context across cloud, identity, application, Kubernetes, and security-monitoring systems.

The potential opportunity is therefore not positioned as "Figma needs another security platform."

The hypothesis is that there may be remaining workflow friction around:

- Cross-system investigation
- Context gathering
- Security-to-engineering handoff
- Investigation of unusual or complex cases
- Manual work required before remediation

---

## 3. Business Problem

### Simulated prospect-confirmed

Complex security investigations can require engineers to manually gather context from multiple systems before determining the appropriate action.

The simulated prospect indicated that Platform Engineering becomes involved when infrastructure changes are required.

---

## 4. Business Impact

### Simulated

The hypothetical discovery conversation estimated:

**10–15 engineering hours/week**

associated with manual investigation and context gathering.

This figure is intentionally classified as **SIMULATED** and should not be presented as a real Figma metric.

Potential business impacts to validate:

- Engineering time consumed by investigation
- Slower investigation resolution
- Security Engineering → Platform Engineering coordination
- Operational overhead
- Difficulty scaling investigation workflows
- Opportunity cost of engineering capacity

## 7. Existing Solution

The prospect has already invested in security investigation automation.

Important implication:

**This is potentially an augmentation/consolidation conversation, not automatically a replacement conversation.**

Questions for AE to validate:

- What does the current solution cover?
- What remains outside the workflow?
- Is there an active evaluation?

## 9. Recommended AE Focus

The AE should validate the commercial significance of the technical workflow issue rather than assume that the technical friction represents a buying opportunity.

Suggested AE questions:

1. How significant is the manual investigation workload?
2. What happens when investigations take longer than expected?
3. Who is affected by the workflow?
4. Is this currently receiving engineering or security leadership attention?
5. What happens if the workflow remains unchanged?
6. Is there an initiative or project associated with improving it?
7. What resources are currently allocated to solving it?
8. Who ultimately approves investment?
9. What alternatives are being considered?
10. What would make this worth changing?

## 11. SDR Assessment

### What we know

- Strong technical ICP fit
- Cloud environment
- Kubernetes environment
- Security engineering function
- Existing security automation
- Potential cross-system investigation friction
- Potential Platform Engineering involvement

### What we do NOT know

- Actual buying intent
- Budget
- Economic buyer
- Decision process
- Competitive environment
- Current vendor satisfaction
- Whether the workflow gap is strategically important
- Whether a new solution is required

### SDR conclusion

**The opportunity is ready for further discovery, not yet ready to be treated as a closed/fully qualified opportunity.**
