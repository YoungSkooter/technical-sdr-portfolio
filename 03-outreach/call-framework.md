# Call Framework

## Objective

The objective of the call opener is to earn enough attention to ask a
technical workflow question.

It is not to deliver a product presentation.

---

## Opener

> "Hi {{Name}}, Skooter here. I know I'm calling out of the blue —
> mind if I take 30 seconds to explain why I reached out?"

If yes:

> "I was looking at some of Figma's security engineering work around
> automating alert investigation and context gathering. I'm curious
> where your team still has to manually investigate or correlate
> information across systems."

Pause.

Let the prospect respond.

---

# If They Say "What Do You Mean?"

> "For example, when an alert comes in, I'm interested in what happens
> between the initial detection and the point where the team knows
> whether it's real, who owns it, and what needs to change."

---

# If They Say "We've Automated That"

> "That's actually what caught my attention. I'm curious what you've
> found is still difficult to automate."

---

# If They Say "Send Me Information"

> "Happy to. So I don't send something irrelevant, is your main focus
> around investigation, cloud-security context, or the remediation
> workflow?"

---

# If They Say "We're Happy With Our Current Tools"

> "Makes sense. I'm not assuming you need to replace anything. I'm
> mainly trying to understand whether there are still gaps around the
> investigation workflow."

---

# If They Are Interested

Transition into discovery:

> "Interesting. Could you walk me through what happens today when one
> of those cases comes in?"

Then stop pitching.

Start discovering.
