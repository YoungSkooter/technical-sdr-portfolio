# End-to-End Technical SDR Simulation

> **SIMULATION — FICTIONAL SALES SCENARIO**
>
> This project is an independent portfolio exercise based on publicly available company and product information. No interaction with Figma employees occurred, and no customer data is represented.

---

# Objective

Demonstrate the complete workflow of a Technical SDR selling into a technically sophisticated B2B organization.

The simulation covers:

```text
Account Research
      ↓
Persona Mapping
      ↓
Technical Outreach
      ↓
Qualification
      ↓
Technical Discovery
      ↓
AE / SE Handoff
      ↓
Product Mapping
      ↓
CRM Management
      ↓
Sequence Analysis
      ↓
Opportunity Decision
