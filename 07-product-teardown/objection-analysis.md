# Objection Analysis

> **PORTFOLIO SIMULATION**

---

## Objection 1 — "We already have a SIEM"

### What the SDR should NOT do

Immediately argue that the SIEM is insufficient.

### Better response

> "Makes sense. I'm not assuming you need to replace it. I'm curious what happens after a cloud-security finding reaches the SIEM. Where does investigation or context gathering still happen?"

### Discovery objective

Understand the workflow boundary.

---

# Objection 2 — "We already have cloud-security tooling"

### Response

> "Understood. What does that tooling cover particularly well today, and where do engineers still need to use other systems?"

### Discovery objective

Identify:

- Coverage
- Gaps
- Workarounds
- Tool overlap
- Satisfaction

---

# Objection 3 — "We built this internally"

### Response

> "That makes sense for a technically mature team. Which parts did you decide were worth building internally, and where does the internal system still require engineering maintenance?"

### Discovery objective

Determine:

- Internal capability
- Maintenance cost
- Coverage
- Engineering ownership
- Strategic importance

---

# Objection 4 — "We're happy with our current solution"

### Response

> "That's good to hear. What part of the current workflow works particularly well?"

Then:

> "Are there any investigation or remediation steps that still require workarounds?"

### Discovery objective

Don't create a problem that doesn't exist.

---

# Objection 5 — "Not a priority"

### Response

> "Understood. Is that because the workflow is already working well, or because improving it isn't currently high enough on the team's priority list?"

### Discovery objective

Distinguish:

```text
No Problem
vs
Problem But Low Priority
