# Interpretation

The simulated LinkedIn sample shows higher engagement rates than email.

However, the sample is small and the audiences were not randomly assigned.

Therefore this cannot establish that LinkedIn will outperform email in general.

---

# Better Testing Method

A real outbound experiment should attempt to control:

- Persona
- Account quality
- Seniority
- Message
- Trigger
- Sequence length
- Time period

Then compare channels.
