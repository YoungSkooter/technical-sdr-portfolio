# Technical Discovery Framework

> **Simulation:** This is a simulated technical discovery exercise.
> Prospect responses are hypothetical.

## Discovery Objective

The objective of technical discovery is to understand the prospect's
actual environment and workflow well enough to determine:

1. What happens today.
2. Where the technical friction occurs.
3. Who is affected.
4. Why it matters.
5. What systems are involved.
6. What an improved workflow would need to look like.
7. Whether a deeper technical evaluation is justified.
